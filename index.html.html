About

Every frameearns its place.

Hi, I’m Sara Jalal, a motion graphics designer who turns ideas into movement. I work with brands, studios and artists who want their story to feel alive on screen, from a three-second logo reveal to a full brand film.

My approach is simple: find one strong idea, give it rhythm, then remove everything that does not move the story forward. Good motion is felt before it is noticed.
